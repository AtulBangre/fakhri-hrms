How TDS flows

1. Employees declare 80C/80D/HRA and choose a regime under My tax.

2. You verify proofs and confirm the annual TDS (or import the CA's sheet).

3. Each payroll run deducts (confirmed − already deducted) ÷ months left in the FY.
