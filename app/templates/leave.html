Rules in force

Paid / sick / casual — count against the leave balance and are paid in full.

Unpaid — no balance impact, but the day becomes loss of pay in payroll.

Half day — a single date only, worth 0.5 day.

Weekly offs & holidays inside a range are skipped, never charged.
