How geo-fencing works

The employee's phone sends its coordinates. The server measures the distance to every active location and marks attendance only when they are inside the circle.

Outside the circle, the attempt is recorded as outside with the distance — but attendance is not marked.

Set the radius generously (200–300 m) so GPS drift does not block honest people. Every check keeps its coordinates, accuracy and optional selfie for review.

Honest limit

A phone's GPS can be mocked. Geo-fencing raises the effort and leaves evidence; it does not prove someone was physically there. Use it for field staff convenience, and review the checks — not as the only control for anything security-critical.
