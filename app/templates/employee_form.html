How this works

1. Save the employee. A code like FITS004 is assigned and a salary revision is recorded.

2. Capture their face. On the employee page, start the camera and take one clear, well-lit photo — front facing, no cap or mask. This is stored in the Face API as a 128-number template, not as a photo gallery.

3. They can scan at the gate. First scan of the day is check-in, the next one is check-out. HR only needs to intervene for exceptions.

Privacy: ask the person before enrolling, tell them what it is used for and delete the enrolment when they leave.
