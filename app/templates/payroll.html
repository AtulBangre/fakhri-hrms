How this month was calculated

Per day rate = monthly gross ÷ {{ basis_days }} {{ 'calendar days' if basis_days > 26 else 'fixed days' }}.

LOP days = absent days + unpaid leave + half-day penalties for repeat late marks.

Earned gross = monthly gross − (LOP days × per day rate).

Net pay = earned gross + bonus − PF − ESI − professional tax − TDS − advance − other deductions.

TDS comes from each employee's tax profile: the CA-confirmed annual figure, less what was already deducted this financial year, spread over the months left.
