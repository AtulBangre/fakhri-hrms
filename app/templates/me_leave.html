How it works

Paid / sick / casual — paid in full and taken from your balance.

Unpaid — no balance impact, but that day is not paid.

Half day — a single date only, worth 0.5 day.

Weekly offs and holidays inside your dates are skipped and never charged.

Who to ask

Your request goes to HR. If it is urgent, message HR directly — the system does not send reminders, so an approved request shows up in your attendance within a minute of HR clicking approve.
