How payable days are counted

Payable = present + (half days × 0.5) + paid leave days. Unpaid leave and unmarked working days in the past count as loss of pay (LOP) and reduce the month's salary in payroll.

Weekly offs and holidays never count against anyone. If someone works on a weekly off, the scan is still recorded so you can give a comp-off.
